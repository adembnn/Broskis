# Style de référence des combattants (validé par Adem, 26/09 12h30)

Rendu cartoon net, en **proportions « jeu »** : tête grosse (≈ 1/4 de la hauteur), corps trapu, grandes mains et grands pieds, formes simples et arrondies faciles à modéliser en 3D. Identité gardée : visage, coiffure, tenue, accessoires.

Modèle : **Nano Banana Pro** (`gemini-3-pro-image`). Entrées : la photo de la personne, puis une image de référence de style si on en a une. **Les photos ne vont jamais dans le dépôt.**

## Prompt « planche du perso » (lot LP2 : vraie 3D)
> Create a character turnaround sheet of the SAME person as in the first photo, keeping their identity: face, hairstyle and hair color, clothes, accessories. Clean cartoon rendering: thick dark brown outline (#2B1D14), flat cel-shaded colors with one shade step, no texture noise. Chunky stylized game-character proportions: big head about one quarter of the total height, short stocky body, short thick limbs, big hands and feet, simple rounded shapes that are easy to model in low-poly 3D. Front view on the left and back view on the right, same scale, relaxed A-pose, full body visible, plain flat light grey background, no text, no ground shadow.

## Prompt « visage sticker » (lot LP : perso en direct)
> Cartoon portrait sticker of the SAME person as in the photo, head and top of the shoulders only, facing the camera, same rendering as a chunky game character: thick dark brown outline (#2B1D14), flat cel-shaded colors, big friendly readable features, keep hairstyle, hair color, glasses or beard if any. Solid pure magenta background (#FF00FF), no gradient, no text, no border.
